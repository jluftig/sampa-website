Hello,

The big news first: addictionpas.org is live — a national home on the web for physician associates in addiction medicine.

Everything on it serves one mission: better outcomes for people and communities affected by substance use disorders, by empowering the PAs who deliver evidence-based, compassionate care.

This letter is the other half of the launch. Once a week — short, useful, and honest about what’s live and what’s still being built.

LIVE TODAY
Daily news for providers
Research, policy, and practice updates — curated for PAs and published every day, so your care stays grounded in what’s happening now.
Read today’s news →
Member networking directory
A private national directory of your peers in addiction medicine. Collaborate, share experience, and feel less alone in the work — you control what’s listed and who can reach you.
Find your peers →
Policy hub for quality care & access
SAMPA’s public voice for expanding access to MOUD and MAT — starting with federal comment letters, growing into positions across federal, state, payment, and practice levers. Evidence-based and non-partisan.

BUILDING NEXT

The site launches with a foundation, not a finish line. In the works:

  • Practice resources — evidence-based guidance PAs can use at the point of care
  • CME — addiction medicine education built for physician associates
  • Job board — mission-aligned openings where PAs can serve people and communities in need

We’re a young organization with a clear ambition — and founding members decide what gets built first. If you believe SAMPA should exist, the best way to make it real is to join.
