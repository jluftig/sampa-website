Society of Addiction Medicine Physician Associates
A national home for addiction-medicine PAs — and a weekly letter worth opening

Hello,

SAMPA exists for a simple reason: better outcomes for individuals and communities impacted by substance use disorders — by advancing the education, practice, and professional community of physician associates who deliver that care.

That work now has a real home on the web: addictionpas.org. This is the first of our weekly updates — short, useful, and honest about what’s live today and what we’re building next.

Building next

This weekly letter is the start of regular member email. We’re also building:

  • Practice resources — evidence-based guidance PAs can use at the point of care
  • CME — addiction-medicine education tailored for physician associates
  • Job board — mission-aligned openings where PAs can serve people and communities in need

We’re a young organization with a clear ambition. Joining now helps us build it.
